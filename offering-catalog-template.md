# Rünna Offering Catalog — Team Fill-Out Template

> This is the menu the Inefficiency Hunter (Phase 2) will pick from. The AI picks
> from THIS list only — it never invents services. So: the sharper this is, the
> sharper (and safer) the pitches.
>
> **How to use:** Copy the blank block at the bottom once per offering and fill it in.
> Keep it punchy. One offering = one buildable thing. Aim for 5–10 offerings total.

---

## What each field is for (read once)

| Field | What to write | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Offering name** | The punchy name we sell it as | Shown to the prospect |
| **One-liner** | Plain-English, what it actually is | Shown to the prospect |
| **Category** | Which pillar it belongs to | Helps the AI group + avoid overlap |
| **Problem it kills** | The specific gap/pain it removes | The AI matches findings to this |
| **Recommend it when…** | The trigger signals (tick boxes below) | This is HOW the AI knows to pick it |
| **What we build** | Concrete deliverables / scope | Sets honest expectations |
| **The outcome** | The result, with a number if possible | The value prop |
| **Proof** | A past client / result / metric | Credibility — keeps claims real |
| **Timeline** | Weeks, not months | Our key differentiator |
| **Investment band** | Rough $ range (INTERNAL only) | For us to qualify — not shown to prospect |
| **The pitch line** | ONE sentence the tool shows | "Here's the system we'd build you…" |

**Fit-signal options to tick under "Recommend it when…"** (these are the exact inputs the Hunter collects):
- **Industries:** e-commerce · restaurant · real estate · pro services · retail · automotive · fitness · hospitality · trades · other
- **Biggest time-sink:** social content · content production · email · leads/follow-up · ads · reporting
- **Website signals (auto-detected):** no Meta Pixel · no analytics · no email capture · no social presence
- **Team size:** solo · small (2–10) · mid (11–50) · large (50+)

**AI Enhancements for Your Business**

### One-liner
Custom AI built into how your business actually runs — automations, smart dashboards,
a sales/prospecting platform, customer-service improvements, and chatbots that learn.
Personalized to you, not an off-the-shelf tool.

### Category
AI & Automation

### Problem it kills
Teams drowning in manual work, slow customer replies, scattered data, and no system
that gets smarter over time. They assume custom AI = a 6–8 month enterprise project.

### Recommend it when… (tick the triggers)
- **Industries:** ✅ all (especially pro services, e-commerce, retail, hospitality)
- **Biggest time-sink:** ✅ reporting · ✅ leads/follow-up · ✅ email
- **Website signals:** ✅ no analytics (no data layer) · ✅ no email capture
- **Team size:** ✅ small · ✅ mid · ✅ large
- Plus: anyone whose pain mentions "manual," "slow replies," "can't keep up," "no visibility"

### What we build (pick what fits the client)
- Workflow **automations** (kill repetitive manual tasks)
- **Smart dashboards** (live view of the numbers that matter)
- A **sales / prospecting platform** (like our own S.P.A.M)
- **CS improvements** — AI that drafts/improves support replies and learns from them
- A **smart chatbot** trained on their business

### The outcome
Hours of manual work removed every week, faster customer response, and a system that
keeps improving — without hiring a dev team or waiting half a year.

### Proof

### Timeline

### Investment band (INTERNAL — not shown to prospect)

### The pitch line (one sentence the tool shows)
